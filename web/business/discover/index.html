04 · YOUR QUESTIONSA LITTLE CLARITY BEFORE YOU START.

Good to know.

Can I use my existing Circum account?

Yes. Sign in with your Circum account, then open the Business Centre to create or join a company workspace.

How do colleagues join our business?

Use the company code to join an existing workspace. Access and permissions are managed through the company’s business roles.

Where can we arrange deliveries?

Business delivery is offered in London and surrounding areas where available. Enter the collection and destination details to check the options and price.

Where do we manage invoices?

Your Business Centre brings together business invoices and payment options. Review the details there before making a payment.
