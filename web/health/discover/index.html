04 · YOUR QUESTIONSA LITTLE CLARITY BEFORE YOU START.

Good to know.

Do I need an existing prescription?

Yes. You are responsible for ensuring the prescription is valid and ready, or will be ready, for collection. Health+ coordinates delivery and does not prescribe or provide medical advice.

Can I arrange regular collections?

Health+ includes one-off and recurring options. Sign in to review the plans and collection choices available for your request.

Where is Health+ available?

Health+ serves eligible collections in London. Availability, collection requirements and pricing are confirmed in the booking flow.

How do I manage a collection?

Sign in to Health+ to view your dashboard and the controls available for your pickups, schedule and payments.
