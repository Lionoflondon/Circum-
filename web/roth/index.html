Is ROTH the same as cash?+

No. ROTH is Circum credit, kept separate from cash earnings. It cannot be withdrawn.

Do I need a new account?+

No. Use your registered email or username. Email verification confirms that you control the account before continuing.

Where will my top-up appear?+

After a successful purchase is confirmed, it will appear in the existing ROTH wallet linked to your verified Circum account.
