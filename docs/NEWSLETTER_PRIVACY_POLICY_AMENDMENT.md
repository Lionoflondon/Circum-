# Proposed newsletter privacy-policy amendment — approval required

This is proposed operational wording only. It is not a published policy version
and must be reviewed and approved by CIRCUM's legal/privacy owner before use.

## Email updates and marketing preferences

Where you choose to subscribe to CIRCUM email updates, we process the email
address you provide, your selected update categories, the source of your
subscription, and records of your consent and preference changes. We use this
information to send the email communications you selected and to maintain
suppression records when you unsubscribe. You can unsubscribe at any time using
the link in an email or manage your preferences using the secure link provided
to you. We retain the minimum consent and suppression history needed to respect
your choice and demonstrate compliance with applicable law.

Before publication, the privacy owner must approve the final wording, retention
period, provider/processor details, international-transfer wording if applicable,
and the policy version/effective date. The application deliberately records no
invented Privacy Policy version until that approval occurs.
