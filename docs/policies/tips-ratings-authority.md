# Tips and ratings policy

Approved by the product owner, 2026-09-06.

Tips are voluntary and separate from delivery fees, cancellation fees, Roth credits and rider compensation. No tip changes dispatch, rank, matching, ETA or acceptance. A confirmed tip on a successfully completed delivery belongs entirely to the rider. It cannot fund Circum fees or cancellation charges.

Before successful completion, cancellation, voiding, failure, unpaid status or refund requires a 100% tip refund/restoration, no rider earning or settlement, and an auditable reversal of any capture. A later delivery-fee refund does not refund a completed delivery's tip. After completion, tip refunds require a specific tip dispute or support/admin approval for duplicate charge, unauthorised payment, fraud, serious service failure, or processor reversal/chargeback. Refunding a tip reverses its rider earning and unpaid payout allocation; already-paid amounts require a recovery/negative adjustment. Preserve immutable audit records and retry idempotency. Never silently retain tips or turn them into cancellation fees.

Ratings policy updated by the product owner: normal written feedback remains Rider-visible. Display average with rating count. Riders can report feedback; support/admin can hide policy-violating comments. Serious issues additionally create a backend-owned support case. No new automatic rank, TP, dispatch, earnings, payout or account penalties. Ratings apply to all completed delivery categories and combinations: Standard, Health+, Gift, Business, Scheduled, Vanguard and Heavy. Project only safe category labels, never private delivery payloads.

Payout allocation is deterministic FIFO across eligible Rider earnings, ordered by earning timestamp then immutable earning ID. Every payout preserves delivery and tip earning identities and exact minor-unit allocations. This does not change earnings totals, the delivery 65/35 split, or eligibility. Tips are exempt from Circum fees and payout fee deductions. Refunds reverse unpaid portions and recover only already-paid portions; every recovery references its original earning and payout allocations. A refunded earning used to settle an earlier recovery restores that earlier recovery's outstanding allocation, without a second balance deduction.

Newcomer acquisition policy approved on 2026-09-06: at most 5 Roth total per newcomer across self-join starter credit and referred-user welcome credit. Credits already issued count even after spending; do not claw back historical grants. The inviter's separately earned referral reward is unchanged. Riders receive no starter gift. Both acquisition ledgers are read atomically before any credit, including concurrent startup/referral retries. The broader held referral PRs are not part of this implementation.
